Neurosurgery

Specialist neurosurgical care for conditions of the brain, spine and nervous system.

Our consultant neurosurgeon offers diagnosis and surgical management of brain tumours, spinal disc disease, hydrocephalus and peripheral nerve conditions, using the latest minimally invasive techniques.

Cardiology

Comprehensive heart care from prevention to advanced intervention.

Our consultant cardiologist manages hypertension, coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias and valvular heart disease with the latest diagnostic and therapeutic approaches.

ENT — Ear, Nose & Throat

Expert care for disorders of the ear, nose, throat and head and neck region.

Services include hearing assessment, sinus treatment, tonsillectomy, rhinoplasty, and management of thyroid and salivary gland conditions.

Urology

Specialist urological care for men and women of all ages.

Our consultant urologist manages kidney stones, prostate conditions, bladder disease, urinary tract infections, male infertility and andrology, supported by in-clinic cystoscopy and ultrasound.

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Complete women's health care from adolescence through to menopause.

Services include antenatal care, high-risk pregnancy management, gynaecological surgery, PCOS, endometriosis, infertility workup and minimally invasive hysteroscopy.

Ophthalmology

Full-spectrum eye care from routine checks to surgical intervention.

Our consultant ophthalmologist manages cataract, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, dry eye, and strabismus, with in-clinic slit-lamp and OCT assessment.

General Surgery

Expert surgical management of a wide range of abdominal and soft tissue conditions.

Procedures include hernia repair, appendicectomy, cholecystectomy, colorectal surgery and wound care, using both open and minimally invasive techniques.

Bariatric Surgery

Life-changing weight loss surgery with long-term follow-up support.

Our bariatric surgeon performs sleeve gastrectomy, gastric bypass and revisional procedures, with a full programme of pre-operative assessment and post-operative nutritional care.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery for faster recovery and less pain.

Advanced laparoscopic procedures across general surgery, urology and gynaecology — smaller incisions, shorter hospital stay, quicker return to normal activity.

Orthopaedics

Specialist care for bones, joints, muscles and sports injuries.

Our orthopaedic consultant manages fractures, knee and shoulder injuries, arthritis, back pain and sports-related conditions, with in-clinic imaging and injection therapy.
