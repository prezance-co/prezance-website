The Centre

Medicine that listens before it treats.

Al Noor was founded on a simple conviction: that good medicine begins with attention. Our consultations are long by design, our investigations are chosen with restraint, and our advice is explained until it is fully understood.

From preventive health to the management of complex, long-term conditions, we bring hospital-grade rigour into a setting that feels considered and calm.

Unhurried by design
Forty-minute consultations as standard.
Evidence first
Investigations guided by evidence, not habit.
One physician

Same-week access
New patients seen within five working days.
